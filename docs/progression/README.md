# Completing Minecraft Java 1.18.1: steps and minimum resources

The configured server version is **Paper / Minecraft Java 1.18.1** (`MC_VERSION=1.18.1`; the prepared runtime log identifies Paper-216 for MC 1.18.1). The goal here is **defeat the Ender Dragon, use the End exit portal, and return through the completion lifecycle**. Minecraft continues afterward. All advancements, Wither, elytra, dragon egg, respawned dragons and full item collection are separate optional goals.

The checklist has **75 milestones** covering the standard survival route, its major legal alternative routes, preparation and optional postgame. Each includes ordered actions, ingredients/minimums, substitutions and the exact automatic evidence check. This is exhaustive within that stated route scope; arbitrary seeds, multiplayer assistance, mods, player skill, loot/RNG and custom experiments make a single universal linear minimum impossible. A rare already-active portal can skip all eye/Nether production. Found equipment can skip the tool chain.

## Preconditions and meaning of minimum

Use a normal generated survival world with structures/strongholds, usable End portal and enabled End travel. Self-sourcing blaze rods ordinarily also needs Nether access, a fortress and a non-Peaceful source of blazes. Paper plugins, disabled dimensions, structure-free superflat/custom worlds, modified recipes/drops or a previously completed world can change feasibility. Starting equipment, keep-inventory, custom structures and no-hunger settings change costs; they are experiment conditions, not secretly completed actions.

Minimum means a conditional ingredient floor, not a guaranteed safe loadout. No fixed universal arrow, block, food, gold-barter or mob-kill number guarantees success. Navigation may lose eyes and combat may consume more supplies. Tools/stations/buckets can be reused, while inserted eyes and exploded beds are consumed. Preparatory tiers are skippable when equivalent items/stations are found. The tracker never requires a skipped alternative before allowing a later milestone to be reached.

## Route and exact resource ledger

Standard route: wood/tools → enough iron/fuel → cast or mine a Nether portal → fortress/rods → powder + pearls (hunt, barter or trade) → eyes → stronghold/portal → End → dragon → exit/credits → return. Tool and pearl branches are alternatives, not tasks that must all be done.

If the portal has s prefilled eyes, P = 12 − s more insertions are needed. If search loses L eyes and no other eye spending occurs, E = P + L eyes must be supplied/crafted: E pearls + E powder, or ceil(E/2) rods. If s=0 and L=0, the insertion-only floor is **12 pearls + 6 rods**. That floor excludes search losses and other material spending. Converting a seventh rod may be sensible preparation, but it is not a universal minimum.

An illustrative self-crafted one-bucket casting bootstrap (not a full guaranteed combat loadout) uses **3 ordinary logs → 12 planks**, spending 4 on table, 2 on four sticks, 3 on wooden pick, then the four sticks between wooden/stone picks; **11 stone-tier drops** for stone pick/furnace; **4 iron ore/raw iron** for bucket+igniter; sufficient smelting fuel and **1 flint**. The remaining three ordinary planks can provide more than four smelts' fuel. Safe water access and ten reusable-bucket lava-source placements make ten frame blocks. Extra weapon, armor, blocks, food and navigational supplies add their own budgets. A reusable table/furnace does not have to be carried afterward.

## Every milestone and its detection

### 1. Overworld foundations

#### 1. Obtain wood — `wood`

**Classification:** route.

1. Find a reachable tree or another legal wood source.
2. Break a log with the attack control, complete mining, and collect the drop.

**Items:** Logs/stems or reusable found planks.

**Minimum:** A normal self-crafted tool route starts with wood; exact total depends on selected tools/fuel.

**Alternatives/limits:** Loot/village workstations or existing equipment can skip wood gathering.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 1 total matching item(s): oak_log, spruce_log, birch_log, jungle_log, acacia_log, dark_oak_log, oak_wood, spruce_wood, birch_wood, jungle_wood, acacia_wood, dark_oak_wood, crimson_stem, warped_stem. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.

#### 2. Obtain planks — `planks`

**Classification:** route.

1. Put a log/stem into the personal 2×2 crafting grid.
2. Take the output; one ordinary log/stem produces four matching planks.

**Items:** 1 log/stem → 4 planks.

**Minimum:** 4 planks for a new crafting table; other recipes consume more.

**Alternatives/limits:** Found/looted planks also satisfy possession, but do not prove crafting.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 1 total matching item(s): oak_planks, spruce_planks, birch_planks, jungle_planks, acacia_planks, dark_oak_planks, crimson_planks, warped_planks. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.

#### 3. Craft planks — `craft-planks`

**Classification:** route.

1. Select a valid log-to-planks recipe, take the result, and clear unused grid items.

**Items:** 1 log/stem per batch of 4 planks.

**Minimum:** One batch when crafting planks yourself.

**Alternatives/limits:** Skip if using found planks.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Received player crafted statistic for oak_planks, spruce_planks, birch_planks, jungle_planks, acacia_planks, dark_oak_planks, crimson_planks, warped_planks at least 1. Cached/pre-existing totals are labeled existing evidence.

#### 4. Obtain a crafting table — `table`

**Classification:** route.

1. Arrange four planks in the 2×2 inventory grid.
2. Take the crafting table, or collect an existing one.

**Items:** 4 planks → 1 crafting table.

**Minimum:** 1 reusable table for recipes needing 3×3.

**Alternatives/limits:** An accessible village/existing table can replace a carried one.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 1 total matching item(s): crafting_table. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.

#### 5. Open a crafting table — `use-table`

**Classification:** route.

1. Place or find a reachable table.
2. Use it to open the 3×3 crafting menu.

**Items:** 1 accessible crafting table; placement does not consume it permanently.

**Minimum:** No new table if one already exists.

**Alternatives/limits:** 2×2 recipes such as eyes do not require a table.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Opened window whose type contains `crafting`; station/menu sighting is not crafting/trade completion.

#### 6. Obtain sticks — `sticks`

**Classification:** route.

1. Place two planks vertically; collect four sticks.
2. Retain unused sticks for later tools.

**Items:** 2 planks → 4 sticks.

**Minimum:** 2 sticks per pickaxe; 1 per sword; 3 per bow.

**Alternatives/limits:** Dead bushes, leaves/loot or found tools can replace crafting.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 1 total matching item(s): stick. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.

#### 7. Obtain a wooden pickaxe — `wood-pick`

**Classification:** route.

1. Use three planks across the top and two sticks down the center.
2. Take and equip the wooden pickaxe.

**Items:** 3 planks + 2 sticks → 1 wooden pickaxe.

**Minimum:** 1 for the conventional stone-tool bootstrap.

**Alternatives/limits:** A found stone-or-better pickaxe skips this tier.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 1 total matching item(s): wooden_pickaxe. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.

#### 8. Mine stone-tier material — `mine-stone`

**Classification:** route.

1. Find exposed stone/cobblestone or deepslate.
2. Mine with a sufficient pickaxe and collect cobblestone/cobbled deepslate.

**Items:** Wooden-or-better pickaxe; suitable block.

**Minimum:** 3 drops for a stone pickaxe; 8 more for a furnace.

**Alternatives/limits:** Loot stone tools or find a furnace instead.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Received player mined statistic for stone, cobblestone, deepslate, cobbled_deepslate at least 1. Cached/pre-existing totals are labeled existing evidence.

#### 9. Hold 11 stone-tier blocks — `stone-material`

**Classification:** route.

1. Collect enough material for a stone pickaxe and furnace.

**Items:** 11 cobblestone/cobbled deepslate, usable by the corresponding recipes.

**Minimum:** 3 + 8 = 11 for these two crafted objects.

**Alternatives/limits:** Possession threshold is a supply check; spent materials may mean this checkpoint is never observed even if later stages succeed.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 11 total matching item(s): cobblestone, cobbled_deepslate. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.

#### 10. Obtain a stone pickaxe — `stone-pick`

**Classification:** route.

1. Craft with three cobblestone/cobbled deepslate and two sticks, or obtain an equivalent higher-tier tool.
2. Use a stone-or-better pickaxe for iron ore.

**Items:** 3 stone-tier blocks + 2 sticks.

**Minimum:** 1 stone pickaxe for the conventional iron route.

**Alternatives/limits:** Found higher-tier pickaxe bypasses this exact tier.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 1 total matching item(s): stone_pickaxe. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.
- Completed received advancement `minecraft:story/upgrade_tools`, evaluated against all transmitted requirement groups.

#### 11. Obtain a furnace — `furnace`

**Classification:** route.

1. Arrange eight cobblestone/cobbled deepslate around an empty center.
2. Take it and place it near a fuel/ore supply, or use an existing furnace.

**Items:** 8 stone-tier blocks → 1 furnace.

**Minimum:** 1 reusable furnace for smelting route.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 1 total matching item(s): furnace. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.

#### 12. Open a furnace — `open-furnace`

**Classification:** route.

1. Use a reachable furnace to access input, fuel and output slots.

**Items:** Accessible furnace.

**Minimum:** No extra material when using existing station.

**Alternatives/limits:** Other accepted iron-smelting station or found ingots can replace it.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Opened window whose type contains `furnace`; station/menu sighting is not crafting/trade completion.

#### 13. Obtain smelting fuel — `fuel`

**Classification:** route.

1. Mine coal or prepare charcoal/other furnace fuel.
2. Put enough fuel in the fuel slot to smelt selected ore.

**Items:** Coal/charcoal, ordinary wood or other valid fuel.

**Minimum:** 1 coal/charcoal smelts up to 8 items; selected bucket+igniter route needs 4 ingots.

**Alternatives/limits:** Nether wood is not ordinary wood fuel; do not assume every plank/log item burns. Looted ingots bypass smelting.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 1 total matching item(s): coal, charcoal. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.

#### 14. Obtain raw iron — `raw-iron`

**Classification:** route.

1. Mine iron/deepslate iron ore with a stone-or-better pickaxe.
2. Collect raw iron drops.

**Items:** Stone-or-better pickaxe and ore.

**Minimum:** 4 raw iron for one crafted bucket plus flint and steel.

**Alternatives/limits:** Found ingots, golem drops or other legal iron sources bypass raw ore.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 1 total matching item(s): raw_iron. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.

**Classification:** route.

1. Place raw iron/ore in furnace input and fuel in fuel slot.
2. Wait for processing, collect ingots, and reserve three for the bucket and one for the igniter.

**Items:** 4 iron ingots; ore/fuel if smelting.

**Minimum:** 4 for this chosen bucket-cast/igniter route; not a universal game minimum.

**Alternatives/limits:** Found buckets/igniter or alternate ignition can lower required ingots.

**Evidence (any applicable rule):**

- Observed player inventory containing 4 total matching item(s): iron_ingot. Possession does not prove crafting or acquisition source.

## Dashboard and evidence semantics

The **Progress** tab shows every milestone, its required/route/preparation/postgame classification, ingredients/minimums and detection. It polls durable progress independently of the policy's selected inputs. All-runs history answers whether any registered agent ever reached a milestone; a run filter scopes it. Reached steps show first observed agent/run, generation/time, evidence source, game mode and existing/setup/live origin. History survives worker/control restart and world/generation resets; it is not current world state. Newly created worlds do not erase past accomplishments.

The default view includes all agent game modes. Survival/adventure filtering uses the recorded mode at each evidence event; it does not certify an unassisted run because setup/gifts can still supply items. A first setup possession is labeled setup, a previously completed advancement/statistic is existing, and later observations are live. A supplied tool reaches Obtain tool but never proves Craft tool. No prerequisite is retroactively marked reached just because a later step succeeded.

Statuses distinguish **Reached**, **Not observed**, and **Awaiting server evidence**. Four procedural/attribution milestones use the read-only Paper plugin for sighting or actor-attributed damage/destruction. They remain unconfirmed until that hook is available; there is no button to fabricate completion. Progress does not implement missing AI actions; current placeholder policies cannot independently complete the game yet. Old inactive runs have no complete retroactive event history, so unknown achievements are not backfilled from logs or guessed from current inventories.

Native tracking listens to this agent's ordinary received advancements/statistics, inventory, dimension, nearby loaded blocks and UI. Statistics use the normal client statistics request, at most once every 10 seconds per native agent, and do not mutate worlds. Progress data is administrative telemetry, not an extra unmasked policy observation. Simulator runs never generate Minecraft milestone evidence. Alternate adapters can implement Environment.watchProgress; missing hooks are shown as unsupported.

Plugin attribution reports a targeted blaze spawner, line-of-sight/forward-facing End crystal, accepted uncancelled player/projectile dragon damage, and crystal removal after that accepted actor damage. It does not mutate the world or expose extra policy sensors. Install with `npm run viewer:build` and restart Minecraft to load it; control must also restart for new routes/workers. Bed explosions with no player-attributed entity damage are not credited as a direct damage event; eventual dragon kill credit still proves combat. Completion/credits remains independently tracked.

## Sources and maintenance

Run `npm run progress:verify` to exercise setup inventory, actual crafting, received advancements, plugin attribution, and the End exit lifecycle against an isolated copy of the prepared Paper runtime. It uses a random port and disposable test world; scripted fixture grants verify detection and are not evidence of a policy beating survival. The normal running server and worlds are left untouched.

Version-specific recipes, advancement predicates and barter counts were checked against the project's cached **official Mojang 1.18.1 server jar**, including `data/minecraft/recipes/ender_eye.json`, `blaze_powder.json`, `data/minecraft/advancements/story/follow_ender_eye.json`, `nether/find_fortress.json`, `end/kill_dragon.json` and `loot_tables/gameplay/piglin_bartering.json`. The installed minecraft-data 1.18.1 registry supplies numeric item/block/entity and packet definitions. No post-1.18 combat items or later smithing recipes are assumed.
